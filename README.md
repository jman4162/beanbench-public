# BeanBench ☕️

**Your complete coffee companion.**
Log your pour overs, dial in your espresso, and track every cafe visit.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758197929)

## Features
- **Perfect Your Pour Over**: Track V60, Chemex, and AeroPress recipes.
- **Master Your Espresso**: Dial in shots with precision (dose, yield, time).
- **Cafe Log**: Remember your favorite shops and beans.
- **Privacy First**: Your data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

## Why "BeanBench"?
Built for the specialty coffee enthusiast, the name combines two core concepts:
1. **The Workbench**: A precision toolset for your craft with timers, logs, and calculators to help you "work" on your technique.
2. **The Benchmark**: The standard of quality you dial in and measure every future brew against.
